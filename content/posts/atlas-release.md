My project, **ATLAS**, has been released and is now available at [atlaslearn.web.id](https://atlaslearn.web.id/).

ATLAS stands for *Atlas, Targeted Learning Assessment System*. It is an academic educational platform for AI-supported adaptive formative assessment.

## A structured path through concepts

ATLAS organises a curriculum around learning objectives, ordered concepts, and configured SOLO levels. Questions follow the concept and level currently assigned in a student's course, with progression tied to demonstrating mastery.

The learning path shown on the site moves through Unistructural, Multistructural, Relational, and Extended Abstract levels. This gives assessment a place within a sequence of learning, with each level building toward the next.

## Feedback that looks at reasoning

An answer is a starting point for feedback. ATLAS provides diagnostic feedback about the response, the reasoning involved, and concepts that may need another look.

For written solutions, the platform presents rubric-based evaluation across conceptual understanding, logical reasoning, use of equations, and clarity of explanation. This makes room to examine how a student arrived at an answer alongside the answer itself.

## An AI assistant grounded in the course

ATLAS also includes a learning assistant for asking questions and exploring concepts. Its explanations are grounded in instructor-provided materials, keeping the assistance connected to the course students are taking.

The platform brings that support together with structured learning paths, formative assessments, feedback, and tools for instructor governance and cohort analytics.

## An invitation to educators and researchers

ATLAS is still at an early stage, so our current priority is research collaboration. We are focusing first on working with educators and researchers to explore its use in teaching and learning and help guide its further development.

If you are interested in using ATLAS in your teaching or research, I invite you to get in touch. I welcome conversations about exploring conceptual learning, adaptive formative assessment, diagnostic feedback, and AI-supported learning in a course setting.

You can reach out through the [ATLAS contact form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScwv4FOLb8NnNO8FelXAOt_2bFHMVh3JCZagyP_OkmvDcI-Eg/viewform?usp=header). Please include a brief introduction, the course or subject you work with, and what you would like to investigate or use ATLAS for. We can discuss how the platform might fit your context and what setup would be needed.
