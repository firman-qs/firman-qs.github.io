{{< cv-entry title="FERRON: Mesin Inversi DEA (Differential Evolution Algorithm) Data Time Domain Electromagnetic" context="Research dataset & HKI-registered software" date="2026" url="https://data.brin.go.id/dataverse/BRIN_ID/?q=Ferron" >}}
A one-dimensional TDEM inversion system for estimating subsurface resistivity and layer thickness using the Improved LPSR Success History Based Adaptive Differential Evolution Algorithm. Developed in **Rust and Python** as a research output from *RIIM Kompetisi Gelombang 8*.
